Dr. Alban — From Oguta to Stockholm: Genesis of a Star

C
Conspodium Culture Desk
September 18, 2026
7 min read
2,154 views
Photo: Live in Stockholm / Afrobeat Europe Archives
How a young Nigerian dentistry student in Sweden revolutionized 90s global dance music and forged a bridge between African rhythm and European pop culture.

In the late 1980s, the bustling streets of Stockholm were at the center of a burgeoning European dance revolution. Into this evolving cultural landscape stepped Alban Uzoma Nwapa—a young man who had arrived from Oguta, Nigeria to study dentistry at Karolinska Institute.

From Dentistry to the DJ Booth

To fund his university tuition, Alban took on DJing gigs at Stockholm's famed Club Alphabet. What began as a nocturnal side-hustle soon transformed into a sonic movement that would define 90s Eurodance across 40 countries worldwide.
